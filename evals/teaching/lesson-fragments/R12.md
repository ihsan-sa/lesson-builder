  <Section title="Series resistance">
    <P>
      Resistors carrying one common current end to end are in series. The same current <M>{"I"}</M>
      produces a voltage drop <M>{"IR_k"}</M> across each, and the drops add around the loop, so the
      combination behaves as a single resistance <M>{"R_s = R_1 + R_2 + \\dots"}</M>. Two 3 Ω resistors in
      series behave as 6 Ω; a 3 Ω and a 6 Ω as 9 Ω.
    </P>
    <KeyConcept label="SERIES RULE">
      Same current, drops add: <M>{"R_s = \\sum_k R_k"}</M>.
    </KeyConcept>
  </Section>
