# Codex GPT-5.6 audit — 2026-07-22
## Verdict
Not release-ready. A six-topic new build can fail before assembly through missing briefs, filename drift, colliding artifact paths, and incompatible specialist manifests.
A small update can erase approved media during splice, and the review loop cannot normalize or apply several declared reviewer outputs.
The policy move reaches fresh bootstraps only; existing workspaces can silently run new lessons without the canonical tutor policy.
